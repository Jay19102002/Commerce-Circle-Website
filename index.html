More Than Coaching — A Premier Commerce Community in Raigarh

Commerce Circle is a dedicated learning community for commerce students in Raigarh, Chhattisgarh. We believe that strong fundamentals, consistent guidance and the right mentorship can transform a student's academic journey.

  • Concept-based learning: Focus on understanding, not cramming.
  • Personal attention & Doubt solving: Every student matters.
  • Exam-oriented preparation & Regular practice: Structured for success.
  • Career guidance & Mentorship: Navigating your professional path.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Everything you need to know about our commerce programs and admission process.

Which classes does Commerce Circle teach?
We provide expert coaching for Class 11 and Class 12 Commerce students across CBSE, ICSE, and CG Board curricula.

Does Commerce Circle offer CA preparation?
Yes, we offer focused learning, conceptual coaching, and mentorship for CA (Chartered Accountancy) Foundation aspirants.

Does Commerce Circle offer CMA preparation?
Yes, we provide preparation and conceptual foundation for CMA (Cost and Management Accountancy) examinations.

Does Commerce Circle offer CS preparation?
Yes, we offer structured preparation and guidance for CS (Company Secretary) aspirants.

Where is Commerce Circle located in Raigarh?
We are located at Gauri Shankar Mandir Rd, near Karnataka Bank & Agroha Bhawan, Raigarh, Chhattisgarh, India 496001. We also have a branch at Durga Chowk, Chakradhar Nagar, Banglapara, Raigarh.
